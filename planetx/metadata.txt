about=A 3D globe in its own window inside QGIS. The whole Earth is shown
      with the OpenStreetMap base map, from space down to single streets.
      Drag the Earth with the left mouse button, it keeps rotating by
      inertia after release. The wheel zooms to the point under the cursor,
      the middle button or Shift with the left button turns and tilts
      the view. Type coordinates in degrees and the camera flies there
      along the van Wijk and Nuij path, rising over long distances and
      landing smoothly.
      Tiles are chosen by screen-space error, the parent stays on screen
      until all its children are loaded, so the base map has no holes while
      zooming. Tiles come through the QGIS network settings and cache.
      Requires OpenGL 3.3 and PyOpenGL, which ships with QGIS on Windows.
      Tested on QGIS 3.36 (Qt 5) and 4.0 (Qt 6).
      Terrain, imagery sources and project layers on the globe are planned
      for the next versions.
      Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
