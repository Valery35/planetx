## Reddit r/QGIS (публикует автор, редактор в режиме Markdown)

Заголовок: PlanetX 1.41 - open national base maps (swisstopo, PDOK, basemap.de, TopPlusOpen) on a 3D globe in QGIS

PlanetX is a free (GPL) 3D globe plugin for QGIS 3.36+ and QGIS 4. Version 1.41 adds open national maps to the Base maps catalog:

- **TopPlusOpen** (BKG) - world map, detailed in Europe, colour and grey
- **basemap.de** - official map of Germany, colour and grey
- **swisstopo** - national map of Switzerland, colour and grey, and SWISSIMAGE aerial photos
- **PDOK** - topographic map of the Netherlands in three styles and aerial photos

A national map is shown inside its country; around it and from afar the globe falls back to an Esri map or imagery, so the rest of the world is not blank.

Map overlays add Waymarked Trails **mountain bike**, **riding** and **ski slope** layers.

- Plugin: https://plugins.qgis.org/plugins/planetx/
- Release and manual: https://github.com/Valery35/planetx/releases/tag/v1.41.0
