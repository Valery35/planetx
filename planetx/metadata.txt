about=A 3D globe in its own window inside QGIS. The whole Earth is shown
      from space down to single streets, with terrain relief and hill
      shading, a glowing atmosphere around the planet and sky at the
      horizon. Esri World Imagery opens by default as an example base map.
      It can be switched to OpenStreetMap or any XYZ Tiles connection set
      up in QGIS.
      The Layers panel adds borders, place names,
      water names, roads with their numbers, railways, airports, rivers,
      lakes, peaks and protected areas from OpenFreeMap vector tiles,
      a coordinate grid, stars with the Milky Way, clouds and land and
      sea temperature from NASA GIBS. 3D buildings from
      OpenStreetMap rise from the ground near the camera.
      The ruler measures ground length and heading, the elevation
      profile of a path shows ascent and slopes. Coordinates show
      in degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, UTM or MGRS. Places
      have icons and a time of their own with a time slider,
      and a tour can be recorded from the screen.
      The Sun row
      lights terrain and buildings by the position of the sun with
      a dark night side, the 3D path and 3D polygon of the ruler
      measure over roofs and walls of buildings.
      The Body icon puts the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, the
      Galilean moons, seven moons of Saturn, Ceres or Vesta in place of
      the Earth, with global NASA and USGS imagery and terrain of Mars
      and the Moon from MOLA and LOLA heights, and opens the starry sky seen from the
      centre of the celestial sphere: the Milky Way, stars,
      constellation lines and names, the Sun, the Moon and the planets
      at the chosen time. The Demo icon with an academic cap opens
      prepared scenes: Perm, Boca Chica and the Japan Trench with a
      section across the subduction zone on the Earth, landing sites
      on Mars and the Moon, Jezero crater on Mars, constellations and bright objects in the
      sky.
      Labels stay upright, do not overlap and hide behind mountains and
      the horizon. The label language follows QGIS or is chosen from
      15 languages and local names.
      Layers of the current project, vector and raster, lie on the globe
      in the map order of QGIS, labels of vector layers stay level like
      city names.
      Drag the Earth with the left mouse
      button, it keeps rotating by inertia after release. The wheel zooms
      to the point under the cursor, the middle button or Shift with the
      left button turns and tilts the view. A double click zooms to a
      point, the right button zooms, Ctrl looks around, arrows, PageUp,
      PageDown, N, U and R move the view. The compass ring,
      look and move sticks and the height slider stay in the corner of
      the view as a faint outline. The globe follows the QGIS map window or leads it, in any
      map coordinate system. A click in the Identify mode lists the
      features of the project layers under the point and selects them on
      the map, selection on the map shows on the globe.
      My Places keep placemarks, paths, polygons and
      measurements in a shared file of the QGIS profile. They are drawn
      by clicks on the globe. The ruler measures lines, paths,
      polygons and circles on the WGS84 ellipsoid. Save view puts a
      placemark that restores the height, heading and tilt. Tours fly
      over My Places and along paths. A snapshot of the view goes to a
      file or into a QGIS layout. KML and KMZ open into My Places and a
      folder saves back to KML. Places rise above the ground and extend
      to it as walls and blocks. Point layers with a time field show as
      growing tracks along the QGIS Temporal Controller. A scene saves
      the whole view to a file, a tour records to PNG frames.
      Type a place name or coordinates in the Search field and the
      camera flies there smoothly, the place found gets a red pin.
      A PDF manual with pictures in Russian and English opens from the
      About window.
      Requires OpenGL 3.3 and PyOpenGL, which ships with QGIS on Windows.
      Tested on QGIS 3.36 (Qt 5) and 4.0 (Qt 6).
      Imagery: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User
      Community. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, vector tiles
      OpenFreeMap. Terrain: Mapzen Terrain Tiles (SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1
      and other sources). Clouds: NASA GIBS, VIIRS. Stars: Yale Bright
      Star Catalogue. Milky Way: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center
      Scientific Visualization Studio, Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
      Mars: NASA, USGS, Viking MDIM2.1. Moon: USGS, LRO LOLA. Tiles of
      both: OpenPlanetaryMap. Mars heights: NASA MGS MOLA MEGDR. Moon
      heights: NASA LRO LOLA GDR. Other bodies: global mosaics of USGS
      Astrogeology and NASA Photojournal from MESSENGER, Magellan,
      Galileo, Voyager, Cassini and Dawn data. Constellations and star names:
      d3-celestial, © Olaf Frohn. Planets: JPL orbital elements.
      Landsat 1-5 MSS images were added after a discussion with Nadezhda
      (https://github.com/nadiopt-cell), the author of QGIS plugins for
      loading satellite images.
      Developed with the support of Inform++ LLC (https://www.informpp.ru/).

      from the NASA sky map. The sky image is downloaded once when the
      stars are first shown. Files of GitHub releases and other
      redirected tiles now load in QGIS 3.
      0.10.0 A coordinate grid: the step follows
      the camera height, lines are labelled, the equator, tropics and
      polar circles are yellow. Stars around the Earth at their places in
      the sky. Clouds from NASA imagery of the last complete day, in the
      Layers panel. Translucent own lines and walls no longer turn dark.
      0.9.0 Copy and paste in My Places: places
      and folders go to the clipboard as KML text, which can be edited in
      a text editor and pasted back. The place properties window no longer
      blocks the globe, changes show at once and a Ground - Space slider
      sets the height. A PDF manual with pictures in Russian and English
      ships with the plugin and opens from About.
      0.8.0 On-screen navigation controls: the
      compass ring with N, the look and move sticks and the height slider
      with plus and minus appear in the top right corner of the view. A
      check box in the view properties keeps them on screen. The view
      properties open from the icon bar. The About window describes the
      features, controls and requirements.
      0.7.0 Mouse and keys: double click zooms to
      a point, right drag zooms, Ctrl looks around, arrows, PageUp,
      PageDown, N, U, R and Space. The left panel has collapsible
      sections Places, Project layers and Layers. Several places are
      selected with Ctrl and Shift, deleted with Del, shown, hidden and
      dragged together. A double click on a project layer flies to it.
      A manual in Russian and English opens from About.
      0.6.1 My Places shows an icon of the kind before each name -
      placemark, path, polygon or saved view. A new placemark gets a
      numbered name at once, My placemark 1, My path 1, View 1, each kind
      counts on its own.
      0.6.0 Scenes: the whole view - camera, time, layers, settings
      and a My Places folder with its tour - saves to a file and opens on
      another computer. Database passwords stay out of the file. The
      record button on the tour bar saves the tour as PNG frames for a
      video, each frame waits for the tiles. The status line shows what
      is still loading and when loading has stalled.
      0.5.2 Growing tracks: Track in the menu of a point layer shows
      it as paths of its objects along the QGIS Temporal Controller, with
      the camera following along the motion if wanted. Labels no longer
      break drawing in QGIS 3.40 with an old PyOpenGL. No errors after the
      globe is closed. A frame no longer stops on an OpenGL error left by
      another plugin.
      0.5.1 The base map no longer stops loading and stays blurred,
      the fault came in 0.5.0. The globe turns as smoothly with large
      places, such as the regions of a country from KML, as without them.
      Place properties: name, description, colors,
      height above ground and extending to the ground, a path becomes a
      wall and a polygon a block. KML files: Open KML or KMZ puts
      the file into My Places with folders, styles and views, Save as KML
      writes a folder back.
      0.5.0 Tours: Play tour flies over the
      checked places of My Places or a folder, a saved view restores its
      height, heading and tilt, along a path the camera travels the line.
      Folders in My Places with dragging of places and folders. View
      snapshot to PNG or JPEG larger than the window, the view into a
      QGIS layout as a picture embedded in the project. Transparency and
      properties of project layers in the globe list. Hide sidebar
      button. Camera motion goes first, tiles load less often while
      moving.
      0.4.3 The plugin passes the Qt 6 check of the QGIS plugin
      repository. Nothing changes in the globe itself.
      0.4.2 My Places is a folder right under the Globe row,
      shown even when empty. Its check box shows or hides
      all places, a double click on a place flies there. Save view is a
      button of its own and puts a placemark that restores the height,
      heading and tilt. The QGIS bookmarks menu is removed.
      0.4.1 A large polygon no longer hangs QGIS while it is drawn.
      Arrow cursor over the globe, closed hand while dragging, cross for
      the ruler, new placemark and identify.
      0.4.0 My Places: placemarks, paths and
      polygons drawn on the globe and saved in a shared file. Ruler with
      line, path, polygon and circle on the WGS84 ellipsoid, measurements
      saved to My Places. QGIS bookmarks and Save view with heading and
      tilt. Selection both ways between the map and the globe. Layers as
      on the QGIS map and new layers straight to the globe. Less detailed
      imagery instead of gray tiles where detailed imagery is missing.
      0.3.2 Synchronization with the QGIS map window in any coordinate
      system. Identify features by a click on the globe. Coordinates and
      elevation under the cursor in the status line. Borders, place names
      and terrain are on by default, other layers off.
      0.3.1 Place search by name, with a red pin
      on the place found and a list of other matches. Search runs through
      Nominatim.
      0.3.0 Esri World Imagery by default. Project layers on the
      globe in the QGIS map order. Layers panel with
      borders, place and water names, roads and road numbers, railways,
      airports, rivers, lakes, peaks and protected areas. Labels
      with a choice of language. Terrain can be
      switched off, vertical exaggeration. Refresh button and automatic
      refresh. Own PlanetX toolbar.
      0.2.0 Terrain relief with hill shading, the camera stays above
      the ground. Atmosphere: glow around the planet, sky and haze at the
      horizon. Base map list with OpenStreetMap and the XYZ Tiles
      connections of QGIS, for example satellite imagery. About window.
      0.1.1 The PlanetX button is now on the Plugins toolbar. The Web
      toolbar, where it was before, is hidden in QGIS by default.
      0.1.0 First release. Globe with the OpenStreetMap base map,
      grab-the-Earth navigation with inertia, zoom to the cursor, turn
      and tilt, fly-to coordinates.
